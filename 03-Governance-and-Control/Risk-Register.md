# Project Risk Register

| Risk ID | Category | Risk Statement | Probability | Impact | Rating | Trigger / Early Warning | Response Strategy | Mitigation Actions | Contingency | Owner | Review Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Risk statement format
**Because of [cause], there is a risk that [event] may occur, resulting in [impact].**

## Response strategies
For threats: **Avoid · Mitigate · Transfer · Accept**  
For opportunities: **Exploit · Enhance · Share · Accept**

## Review questions
- Has probability or impact changed?
- Is the response reducing exposure?
- Has a trigger occurred?
- Does the risk require escalation or contingency activation?
- Has a new risk emerged from a change, dependency or decision?
