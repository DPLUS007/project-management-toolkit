## Change assessment
For each material change:
1. Clarify the requested outcome.
2. Assess scope and requirement impacts.
3. Identify schedule, resource, cost, risk and quality effects.
4. Identify ERP/COTS configuration, integration, data and vendor implications where relevant.
5. Obtain the required approval before changing the baseline.
6. Update affected plans, requirements, RAID, testing and communications.
