# User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Plan

## Purpose
Confirm that the solution supports approved business requirements and is ready for business use.

## Scope
**In scope:**  
**Out of scope:**  

## Roles
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Business owner | Approves business acceptance |
| UAT lead / PM | Coordinates plan, progress and escalation |
| Business testers | Execute scenarios and record evidence |
| Technical / Vendor team | Investigate and resolve defects |
| SMEs | Clarify expected business behavior |

## Entry criteria
- [ ] Requirements and acceptance criteria approved
- [ ] Test environment available and stable
- [ ] Required data and access available
- [ ] Critical system/integration testing complete
- [ ] UAT scenarios reviewed
- [ ] Testers identified and briefed

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Defect ID | Severity | Description | Owner | Target Fix | Retest Result | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Exit criteria
- [ ] Critical scenarios passed
- [ ] Critical/high defects resolved or formally accepted
- [ ] Residual risks documented
- [ ] Business owner acceptance recorded
- [ ] Open items transferred to an owned action/support plan
