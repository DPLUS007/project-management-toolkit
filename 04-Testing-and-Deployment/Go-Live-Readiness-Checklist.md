# Go-Live Readiness Checklist

## Solution
- [ ] Approved scope is delivered or exceptions are documented.
- [ ] Critical requirements are traceable to validation evidence.
- [ ] Integrations and interfaces are production-ready.
- [ ] Access/security requirements are complete.

## Data
- [ ] Migration approach and ownership confirmed.
- [ ] Reconciliation/validation completed.
- [ ] Outstanding data issues have owners and decisions.

## Testing
- [ ] UAT exit criteria met.
- [ ] Critical/high defects resolved or formally accepted.
- [ ] Regression/retest requirements completed where applicable.

## Business readiness
- [ ] Training completed for affected users.
- [ ] Process/procedure changes communicated.
- [ ] Business owners understand known limitations and workarounds.
- [ ] Support contacts and escalation routes communicated.

## Operations
- [ ] Monitoring/support responsibilities assigned.
- [ ] Hypercare coverage confirmed.
- [ ] Vendor support/SLA path confirmed.
- [ ] Handover documentation available.

## Governance
- [ ] RAID reviewed.
- [ ] Cutover plan approved.
- [ ] Contingency/rollback criteria agreed.
- [ ] Go/no-go decision recorded.
