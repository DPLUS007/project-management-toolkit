# ERP / COTS Fit-Gap Register

Use this register to separate business needs from solution assumptions and make implementation decisions visible.

| ID | Process Area | Business Requirement | Standard Capability | Gap / Constraint | Classification | Proposed Resolution | Business Owner | Vendor / Technical Owner | Impact | Decision Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Classification guidance
**Standard** — adopt delivered functionality.  
**Configure** — meet the need using supported settings/workflows.  
**Integrate** — connect ERP/COTS to another system or data source.  
**Process change** — adjust the business process to align with the product.  
**Extension / customization** — additional development or non-standard capability; assess cost, supportability and risk.  
**Further review** — more analysis or stakeholder decision required.

## Decision considerations
- Business value and regulatory/operational need
- Total cost and schedule impact
- Upgrade/support implications
- Data and integration dependencies
- Security and access implications
- Testing effort
- Training and change impact
- Vendor responsibility and support model
