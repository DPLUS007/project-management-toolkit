## ERP & COTS implementation focus

ERP and packaged/COTS delivery requires more than tracking a schedule. The project team must connect business requirements to standard product capability, make fit-gap decisions, coordinate vendors and integrations, validate the solution with users, and prepare the organization for transition.

This toolkit therefore emphasizes:

- Requirements and process discovery
- Fit-gap classification and solution decisions
- Vendor/COTS evaluation
- Integration, data, access and environment dependencies
- RAID and risk governance
- Change control
- Requirements traceability
- UAT and defect management
- Business and operational readiness
- Cutover, contingency and go/no-go
- Hypercare and handover

## How I use project artifacts

I treat project documentation as a **delivery control system**, not administrative paperwork.

A useful artifact should answer a delivery question:

- **Charter:** Why are we doing this, what is included, and who owns success?
- **RACI:** Who is responsible and who has decision accountability?
- **RTM:** Can we trace business needs through solution and validation?
- **Fit-Gap Register:** How will the ERP/COTS product satisfy each business need?
- **RAID/Risk:** What could prevent delivery and who owns the response?
- **Change Log:** What is changing and what does it affect?
- **Status Report:** What needs management attention now?
- **UAT Plan:** Has the business demonstrated that the solution works for its needs?
- **Cutover Plan:** Are people, technology, data and support ready to transition?
- **Lessons Learned:** What should future projects repeat or change?
